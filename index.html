Injury Care & Recovery Guide

Protect your body with smart recovery techniques. Learn the basic first-aid steps for common workout injuries and know when it's time to seek professional medical help.

Warning

Stop exercising immediately if you experience severe pain, dizziness, heavy swelling, difficulty breathing, or suspect a fracture. Seek medical assistance as soon as possible.

Rest & Ice Therapy

Rest the injured area and apply an ice pack for 15–20 minutes every few hours during the first 48 hours to reduce swelling and pain.

⏱ 20 Min ❄ Reduce Swelling

Compression Support

Wrap the injured area with an elastic bandage. The bandage should be firm but not tight enough to block blood circulation.

🩺 Support 💪 Stability

Elevation

Keep the injured limb elevated above your heart level whenever possible to reduce swelling and improve recovery.

🛌 Rest ⬆ Better Recovery

Consult a Doctor

Visit a healthcare professional if pain becomes severe, swelling increases, numbness appears, or movement becomes difficult.

👨‍⚕ Medical Help 🚑 Safety First

💡 Quick Recovery Tips

  • Stay hydrated throughout the day.
  • Avoid intense workouts until fully recovered.
  • Stretch gently before returning to exercise.
  • Eat protein-rich foods to support muscle repair.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours for faster recovery.

Your Health Comes First ❤️

Recover smart, train safely, and always listen to your body. Prevention is better than treatment.
