FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much protein should I eat daily?

Most adults need 0.8–1.2g of protein per kg of body weight. Athletes may require more.

How much water should I drink?

Generally 2–3 liters per day, depending on activity level and climate.

Is breakfast important?

Yes, a healthy breakfast provides energy and improves concentration throughout the day.

Can I lose weight without exercise?

Weight loss is possible through calorie control, but exercise improves overall results.

Which foods are rich in protein?

Eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, lentils, tofu and dairy products.
